R16 (the controller's ruling, issued after Task 4 was committed): a bind confirmation keeps the buttons until the game takes the roll. Need or Greed on a soulbound item makes the game fire `CONFIRM_LOOT_ROLL(rollID, rollType)` and show Blizzard's dialog; the roll is not made until it is accepted. `Pick` still puts the choice in `card.mine` before it calls `RollOnLoot` (a new pick clears `card.pending`). On the confirmation, the choice the game names (rollType 1 need, 2 greed, 4 transmog; else the card's `mine`, else an earlier `pending`) moves from `mine` to `card.pending`; `CARD_CHANGED` carries `mine`, so a listener must read `card.mine`, which is nil then, and `AwaitsMe` is true again: the buttons work while the dialog is up, and a cancelled dialog fires nothing and leaves the card awaiting the player. On `CANCEL_LOOT_ROLL` (and for each card on `CANCEL_ALL_LOOT_ROLLS`) `pending` becomes `mine`, in the same `CARD_CHANGED` as `over`. Tutti never calls `ConfirmLootRoll`: the dialog stays with Blizzard. A confirmation for a card whose part is over, for no card, or with an unreadable roll ID is ignored; one for a card with no pick on it (rolled elsewhere) keeps the choice as `pending` and announces nothing, as nothing changed. Known limit: if the time runs out while the dialog is open, `pending` becomes `mine` although the game passed; Task 5's history (the `isSelf` entry wins for the player's row) corrects it. — a player left without buttons by a dialog they cancelled is the anti-goal "a roll missed because of Tutti" — cost if wrong: a `mine` the history corrects.

R18 (the controller's ruling, issued after Task 5 was committed): what the rows show. The player's row, in this order: the history's `isSelf` entry when its state is decided (need, greed, transmog or pass), with its roll; else `card.mine` (the click shows at once, even while the history still says NoRoll for the player); else "wait" when the history has a NoRoll entry for the player or the card has a `rollID` for them; else "out" once `card.history` (the player is not in this roll, as for any member); else "wait". The same order serves a player the group list could not list (the history's `isSelf` row). On a card whose status is "decided" or "ended" every row still at "wait" reads "out" (the leavers and the player included): nobody is choosing after the end; a card made by hand with no status is left alone. — the player's click must not flicker back to choosing, a roll the player is not part of must not say "choosing", and the end state must not leave pulsing dots — cost if wrong: R16's timeout case shows the pick the game passed on when the history says NoRoll (the history corrects it only when it says Pass); an ended card that never had the history shows everyone but the player's own click as out.
Ruling: `changes.picks[guid]` is "state" when the pick is new or its state changed (a number that arrives with it is part of that) and "roll" when only the number is new or different; a pick is never taken away (a member who leaves keeps theirs); a state or a field the game keeps secret leaves the one the pick had; an update that changes nothing sends no `CARD_CHANGED`, except a card's first sight of its drop, which always does — cost if wrong: the card view fades a pick or spins a number once too often or too seldom.
